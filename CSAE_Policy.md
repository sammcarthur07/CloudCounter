# Child Safety Standards and CSAE Policy
## CloudCounter Application

**Last Updated: September 2025**

### Overview
CloudCounter is a habit tracking application designed exclusively for adults aged 18 and above. We are committed to maintaining a safe environment and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) content on our platform.

### Age Requirements
- **Minimum Age**: 18 years old
- **Verification**: App is rated for 18+ in Google Play Store
- **Target Audience**: Adults seeking habit tracking and cessation support tools

### Safety Measures

#### User Authentication
- Users can optionally sign in using Google authentication
- Social features requiring Google sign-in include:
  - Chat messaging between users
  - Video calls (cloud rooms)
  - Shared stash features
- Anonymous users have access to personal tracking features only

#### Content Reporting System
Users can report inappropriate content or concerning behavior through:
- **In-App Reporting**: Long-press any message to report directly
- **Email Reports**: Reports are sent to mcarthur.sp@gmail.com
- **Response Time**: All reports are reviewed within 48 hours
- **Action Taken**: Violating accounts may be suspended or terminated

#### Prohibited Content
The following content is strictly prohibited:
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
- Content sexualizing minors
- Grooming behavior or inappropriate contact with minors
- Any content that exploits or harms children

### Reporting Obligations

#### Legal Compliance
CloudCounter complies with all applicable laws regarding CSAE, including:
- Immediate reporting of CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)
- Cooperation with law enforcement investigations
- Preservation of evidence related to CSAE reports

#### Report Handling Process
1. User reports content via in-app reporting feature
2. Report is immediately forwarded to designated safety contact
3. Content is reviewed within 48 hours
4. If CSAE content is identified:
   - Content is immediately removed
   - User account is suspended
   - Report filed with appropriate authorities
   - Evidence preserved for law enforcement

### Data Protection

#### Data Retention
- Reported content is preserved indefinitely for legal compliance
- User data is retained while account is active
- Users can delete their account and associated data at any time
- CSAE-related reports and evidence are retained as required by law

#### Privacy Protection
- User reports are handled confidentially
- Personal information is only shared with authorities when legally required
- Firebase security rules protect user data from unauthorized access

### Contact Information

**Designated Safety Contact**
- Email: mcarthur.sp@gmail.com
- Response Time: Within 48 hours
- Available for questions about our CSAE prevention practices

**Emergency Reporting**
For immediate safety concerns, users should contact local law enforcement.

### Transparency and Accountability

#### Regular Reviews
- Safety policies reviewed quarterly
- Reporting mechanisms tested regularly
- Staff trained on CSAE identification and reporting

#### User Education
- Clear community guidelines available in-app
- Reporting instructions easily accessible
- Regular reminders about safety features

### Compliance Statement
CloudCounter affirms its commitment to:
- Preventing CSAE content on our platform
- Complying with all applicable child safety laws
- Reporting to regional and national authorities as required
- Maintaining a safe environment for all users

### Updates to This Policy
This policy may be updated to reflect changes in laws, regulations, or our safety practices. Users will be notified of significant changes.
